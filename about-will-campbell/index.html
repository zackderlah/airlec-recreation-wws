About Airlec Australia

What We Do

Airlec Australia specialises in the supply and installation of affordable solar power, air conditioning and general electrical services. Our company provides services to clients in both South East and Western Queensland and is committed to delivering quality products and services and tailoring solutions for customers that are best suited to their needs. We take pride in delivering a superior customer experience by offering an excellent standard of workmanship and industry knowledge, quality products and safe working procedures and practices.

Exceptional Solar and Electrical Solutions

Airlec Australia will provide exceptional solar, air conditioning and electrical solutions for our customers in line with current workplace health and electrical safety standards and regulations. We strive to build long term and repeat clientele by not only meeting, but exceeding our client’s needs and expectations and by providing a prompt and responsive service that values quality and integrity. Airlec is committed to being ahead of the field by keeping up to date with industry changes, new products and technologies to be able to pass onto our client base.
