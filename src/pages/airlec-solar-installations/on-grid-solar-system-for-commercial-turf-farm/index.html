Airlec Solar Installations

On-Grid Solar System for Commercial Turf Farm

1/4/2020

This project consisted of adding two additional systems to supplement the high-power usage at the turf farm. The systems that were installed consisted of:

  • 6.82 KW system consisting of 22 x Winaico WST310-M6 panels coupled with a SolarEdge Inverter SE7K
  • 17.36 KW system consisting of 56 x Winaico WST310-M6 panels coupled with a SolarEdge Inverter SE15K

The Winaico Panels hold a 15 year product warranty and 25 year performance warranty with the SolarEdge inverters offering superior efficiency ratings with a 12 year warranty extendable to 25 years.

To find out more about these panels click on the link below:

https://www.winaico.com.au/products/wst-310-m6

To find out more about the Solar Edge three phase inverters click on the link below:

https://www.solaredge.com/aus/products/pv-inverter/three-phase#/
